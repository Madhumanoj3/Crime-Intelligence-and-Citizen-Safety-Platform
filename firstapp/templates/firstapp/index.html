How It Works

1

Citizen Reports

A citizen files a complaint with the category, location and details.

2

AI Analyses

The complaint is routed to its station, checked for duplicates and given a predicted priority.

3

Inspector Validates

The station inspector reviews the AI advice, validates the complaint and assigns an officer.

4

Officer Investigates

The officer investigates, files the FIR and updates the case. The citizen tracks progress live.

About the Platform

The Crime Intelligence and Citizen Safety Platform bridges the gap between citizens and law enforcement agencies. It provides a simple and accessible way to report incidents, track complaints, and stay informed about community safety.

The platform serves four user roles: Citizens report complaints and emergencies, Station Inspectors validate complaints and assign officers, Police Officers investigate cases, and Administrators oversee the entire system.
