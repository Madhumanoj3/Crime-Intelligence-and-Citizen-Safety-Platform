About the Platform

The Crime Intelligence and Citizen Safety Platform is designed to bridge the gap between citizens and law enforcement agencies. It provides a simple and accessible way to report incidents, track complaints, and stay informed about community safety.

This platform serves three user roles: Citizens can report complaints and emergencies, Police Officers can manage cases and investigations, and Administrators can oversee the entire system.
